Once upon a time, a beautiful princess named Snow White fled her jealous stepmother, who was consumed by vanity. Deep in the dark forest, Snow White found a welcoming cottage belonging to seven kind dwarfs.

Learning she had survived, the wicked queen disguised herself and tricked the princess into biting a poisoned apple, causing an eternal sleep. The grieving dwarfs placed her in a glass coffin until a traveling prince woke her with a loving kiss, and they lived happily ever after.
